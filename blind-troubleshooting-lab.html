Blind Troubleshooting Lab

Investigate a fictional lab system without being told the cause. You may inspect state and make controlled corrections; an incorrect fix does not count as success. Everything is simulated locally in your browser. No shell, network or physical device is accessed.

Assignment

The training portal is unavailable. Determine why, restore access, and prove the problem is resolved. Four independent hidden configurations are available; the initial case is selected automatically and is not labeled by fault type.

Diagnostic terminal

student@lab:~$

Assess your work

Required: inspect at least two different observations, select a correction, and verify portal connectivity. The rubric rewards investigating the cause rather than guessing. Type grade-lab to see your result.

Scores are independent of prior Academy exams. Lab results provide simulation evidence, not proof of real-system proficiency.
