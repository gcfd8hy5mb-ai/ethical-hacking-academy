Real-System Lab Field Guide

Begin with instructions on your iPhone, then perform read-only diagnostics on your own Windows or isolated Linux VMs. These are real-system *instructions*, not connected remote-control tools. No commands are executed by this page.

Safety and scope: Only inspect owned or explicitly authorized machines. Use disposable isolated VMs for any changes. Never scan other households, work networks, or internet addresses; a sample output is not a claim about your computer. You may complete the reading and simulation portions on a phone and do the real practical work later.
