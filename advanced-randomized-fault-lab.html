Levels 6–8 · Independent diagnosis

Randomized Fault Investigation

Each scenario selects two different faults from three possible conditions. You will need to inspect simulated outputs, decide what is wrong, apply the appropriate simulated repairs, and verify restoration. A new attempt changes the combination and order.

No system commands execute on your actual phone, computer, network, or cloud environment. All repair operations are fictional training actions. Your existing exam and Academy progress records are untouched.

Objective: determine and correct both faults without assuming the first successful repair resolves the entire incident.

Investigation terminal

Restoration checks actual simulator state. Written notes are saved but not scored for correctness; passing a simulation does not establish practical competence.
