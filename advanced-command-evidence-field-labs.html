Command & Evidence Field Labs

Five detailed, four-stage labs covering Linux, Windows, identity review, detection engineering and enterprise incident response. For each stage learn what a command does, why to use it, how to interpret expected output, and what evidence to keep.

Displayed terminal output is fictional training material, not a live terminal. Run commands only on systems you own or are expressly permitted to test. Checkpoint answers are locally saved and are not professional skill certification.
