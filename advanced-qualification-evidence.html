Record what needs correction; changes here do not rewrite an assessor's grades. Re-import a newly reviewed report after a retest to refresh results.

Qualification rules

To reach provisionally reviewed status, each Level 6, 7 and 8 needs an assessor-identified, attested report with all 16 rubric criteria scored, at least 52 of 64 points, no zero criteria, and an observed adequate result in an authorized lab. Missing evidence, remediation or unobserved exercises prevent provisional readiness. These are internal practice gates, not validated academic or professional standards.

Formal credentialing and independent competency sign-off are not provided by this app. Recorded scores and report files can be falsified; an independent review must verify the assessor, raw evidence and laboratory demonstration.
