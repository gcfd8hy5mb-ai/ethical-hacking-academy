Levels 6–8 · Simulated command environments

Advanced Stateful Workstations

Five interactive troubleshooting environments. Commands inspect or change simulated system state, allowing you to test hypotheses, repair a fault, and verify results. They do not run terminal commands on your device or network.

Use these as preparation for later supervised labs on systems you own or have explicit written authorization to assess. Solving a simulation is not a professional qualification.
