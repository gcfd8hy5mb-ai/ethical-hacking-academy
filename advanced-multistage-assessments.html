Advanced Levels 6, 7 & 8

Multi-stage evidence assessments

Three four-stage investigations require independent reasoning, safe test interpretation, documented uncertainty, and professional written reports. Complete stages in order. A draft of at least 180 characters unlocks the next stage; this is a length gate, not automatic grading.

Drafts and self-review are stored only on this device. No original lesson completions, exam grades or cloud records are modified. A qualified assessor must independently verify real-world competence.

Assessor instructions

Require the candidate to reproduce approved tests on their own isolated systems, explain what each tool does and why it was chosen, identify contradictory evidence, and deliver clear records. The checklist is self-reported and cannot independently certify proficiency. Do not use real credentials, public targets, or unauthorized infrastructure.
