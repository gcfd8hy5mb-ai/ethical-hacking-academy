Investigation mastery checklist

  • Distinguish symptoms from likely causes before replacing parts.
  • Use exact model-specific manuals for hardware repairs and electrical safety.
  • Change one variable at a time and preserve a rollback path.
  • Validate isolation before running tools against vulnerable training VMs.
  • Document evidence and retest after remediation.
No live scanning or exploitation is performed. Never open power-supply housings; power off and isolate equipment before internal service. Keep intentionally vulnerable virtual machines isolated from your household and public networks.
