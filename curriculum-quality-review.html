Review criteria

For each criterion, 0 = missing, 1 = partial, 2 = clear and independently usable. A lesson passes this editorial gate at 12/14 with no zero in technical accuracy or authorization.

Editorial versus practical verification

A high rubric score cannot prove the commands work on a real machine. Technical verification requires approved lab execution, reproducible outputs, negative and positive tests, independent review, and a versioned correction record. No lab execution or external reviewer sign-off is performed here.
